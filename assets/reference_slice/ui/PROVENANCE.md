# Reference slice UI assets

2026-09-24. Opt-in approval candidate; full rollout and owner approval remain pending.

Six equipment sprites were generated as original project artwork using the built-in OpenAI image-generation tool: **Overdrive Driver, Sand Cleats, Coin Magnet, Rangefinder Lens, Heavy Core, Lucky Putter**. They are AI-generated artwork, not claimed hand-drawn assets or extracted reference art. The exact generation requests are retained in `source/generation_prompts.json`; full source PNGs remain unchanged under `source/`, excluded from Godot import by `.gdignore`. No Grandpa Golf image or extracted pixels were supplied as an edit target.

`tools/reference_slice/ui/export_ui_assets.py` thresholds alpha at 112, trims transparent margins, preserves aspect ratio, and exports with nearest sampling into 128 px transparent equipment canvases and 32 px rail canvases. This tooling does not construct the illustrated equipment. `export_manifest.json` records source dimensions, crop rectangles and exported SHA-256 values. Generated full-size files contain detail finer than the selected game scale; actual 128 px export inspection is the quality evidence.

The exporter authors eleven small 40 px stepped UI frames: thin 2 px warm rims, selective dark outer edges and translucent quiet centers. These are functional UI artwork. The 32 px rating star is an original semantic icon authored in the same script, used for the existing resolved star rating without inventing rewards. The 32 px wallet coin is cropped from this project's generated Coin Magnet illustration; its exact crop and hash appear in the export manifest. No extracted reference asset is shipped.

Typography uses **Jersey 10 Regular**, by Sarah Cadigan-Fried / The Soft Type Project Authors (Copyright 2023), downloaded unmodified from the [official Google Fonts repository](https://github.com/google/fonts/tree/main/ofl/jersey10) on 2026-09-24. Its [SIL Open Font License 1.1](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/google/fonts/main/ofl/jersey10/OFL.txt) permits bundling and embedding under its conditions; the full notice and license are retained at `fonts/Jersey10-OFL.txt`. The unmodified font SHA-256 is `DB9CBD091617048A145D249DAA2B815FE7083BE6AB66AC26626E21A4E01C3E82`. Existing `assets/fonts/AtkinsonHyperlegible-Bold.otf` remains the supporting long-copy font under its already bundled OFL. The reference font's identity is not asserted.

The first integrated candidate used Pixelify Sans. Actual-size specimens comparing that family at weight 700 with Jersey 10 and Jersey 15 appear under `artifacts/reference_review/ui/font_comparison_1080.png` and `font_comparison_720.png`. Jersey 10 was selected for compact, substantial display letterforms, then inspected in integrated 720p and 1080p captures. Jersey 15 was an analysis-only candidate retained with its license outside production assets. Automated rendering and agent review do not establish owner approval.

The approved main logo is referenced directly from **`assets/pixel_sample/ui/wordmark.png`**. Its source is `assets/pixel_sample/source/wordmark.png`; the existing export crop is documented in `tools/pixel_sample/export_assets.py` and `assets/pixel_sample/export_manifest.json`. Neither source nor exported logo is regenerated, recolored or edited.
