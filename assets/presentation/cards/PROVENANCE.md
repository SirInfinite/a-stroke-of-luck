# Production cartoon card illustrations

2026-09-24. Original project artwork recovered and extended for the owner's production visual correction. No reference-game sprite, extracted media, paid asset or external illustration is included.

Four drawings are exact quadrant crops of the preserved project source `assets/pixel_correction/source/cards_cutout.png`: orange **Overdrive Driver**, yellow **Sand Cleats**, red **Coin Magnet**, and violet **Rangefinder Lens**. This earlier cartoon family was visually distinguished from the realistic scene illustrations in `assets/pixel_sample` and the later detailed equipment in `assets/reference_slice/ui`.

Four matching drawings were generated with the built-in OpenAI image-generation tool using only the recovered project sprites as style references: **Heavy Core** (steel golf-ball cutaway with gold core), **Lucky Putter** (green mallet, clover and ball), **Power Club** (coral iron with power chevrons), and **Gust Guard** (teal golf umbrella deflecting wind). These are AI-generated original project assets; this record does not claim human pixel authorship or exclusivity. Full prompts and local generation filenames are retained in `source/GENERATION_PROMPTS.json`.

An actual-size review found that the first Power Club and Lucky Putter drawings devoted too much area to their shafts, leaving the heads small at 32 pixels. Both sources were edited with image generation to shorten shafts/grips and enlarge the heads while preserving colors, object identity, full ends and surrounding padding. The initial drawings remain in `source/history/`; `composition_refinement.png` records the material before/after at the same display sizes. This is a source composition change, separate from the square export fit.

The raw recovered/generated originals remain in `source/originals/`. `tools/build_card_art.py` produces 1024×1024 editable square masters in `source/` and eight 80×80 runtime PNGs directly in this directory. A complete silhouette occupies at most 896 source pixels / 70 runtime pixels, leaving at least five runtime pixels of transparent gutter on every side. The exporter checks that the original object's alpha never touches its source boundary; padding cannot conceal a genuinely truncated source. It thresholds alpha, preserves the authored color design, fits uniformly and resamples with nearest neighbor. It does not synthesize or redraw geometry. Generated shading is deliberately grouped but not claimed to be a strict indexed palette.

All four recovered cartoon sources are already complete. Inspection also found no source-edge truncation in the six later isolated `reference_slice` sources. The earlier narrative scenes crop contextual body/equipment portions; the comparison evidence labels those as different source compositions. No false claim of inpainting or a missing-part repair is made.

`source/.gdignore` keeps large editable originals out of the Godot import graph. Runtime code references only `res://assets/presentation/cards/<card_id>.png`; no tools, artifacts or sample assets are required at runtime. `export_manifest.json` records exact source/master/runtime hashes, dimensions, crop bounds and byte sizes. `python tools/build_card_art.py --check` verifies reproducibility without changing assets.

The native `IconCatalog` exposes the original eight card IDs, and the inherited tutorial aliases remain: Training Driver → Overdrive Driver, Practice Sand Shoes → Sand Cleats, Pocket Change → Coin Magnet, Steady Grip → Rangefinder Lens. `CardIllustration` and card-category icons draw the same square full-color artwork without a tint, stock symbol or medallion. Rarity and purchase state remain responsibilities of the enclosing controls. Card definitions, benefits, curses, prices and stacks are unchanged.

Source-scale, actual 32/80/160-pixel, and source-history comparison sheets live under `artifacts/production_visual/cards/`. They are local review evidence, not runtime assets. Native shop/HUD/details review and repository verification are recorded by the integration lead.
