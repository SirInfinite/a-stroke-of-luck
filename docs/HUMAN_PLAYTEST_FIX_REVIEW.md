- Native RigidBody shape CCD was insufficient at boosted diagonal contacts and extreme corners. One failure involved an impossible native positional-separation jump after contact. `BallMotion` sweeps completed travel, resolves earliest impact and remaining time with an eight-impact cap; GolfBall bounds anomalous position displacement by actual velocity/delta before the sweep. Normal native dynamics, impulse and material tuning remain unchanged. Pad exit separation also sweeps; boundary art/collision now includes square corner fills.
- The old forecast used a different damping equation/order and missed world damping. Prediction now shares Godot's discrete damping equation and the static sweep, simulates until the same stopped-frame condition, and accounts for known sand/ice and Area2D's one-step notification delay. Terminal arrow tip is the simulated stop. Nine low/medium/high × power-modifier cases had 0.000 px error; three static-bank cases had a maximum 3.391 px error; four actual sand/ice cases had 0.000 px error. Regression tolerances: 12 px open course, 18 px banks/surfaces (100 px tiles). Current-elevation assumption is explicit: moving hazards, ramps, wind and pads can change the forecast.
- RunState issues one monotonic ticket per accepted shot. Automatic three-second OOB recovery consumes only that eligible ticket on the same active hole, decrementing hole and total strokes once. Earlier shots and water/lava/crush/manual-reset costs remain. Resolution/history and the par+4 warning use the refunded value. Completing inside or taking a normal hazard/manual reset expires the ticket.
- Two separate position/rotation assignments on a synchronized AnimatableBody could discard pendulum translation. One atomic transform now moves spiky art, body and detector together. Telegraph chain reads the real hazard. Tests observe live physics cycles, not just direct method calls.
- Ice's size override was also silently ignored by Dictionary.merge's default non-overwrite behavior. Generated definitions, validator, runtime collision, telegraph and landed art now agree on one 100×100 tile. Reset/rebuild clears the persistent block and re-arms its shadow.
